# Security policy

Please report vulnerabilities privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting ("Report a vulnerability" on the Security tab), not in public issues.

Fallback if private vulnerability reporting is unavailable: email **TODO-OWNER: add security contact email here** (placeholder, not yet set). Do not include exploit details in public issues either way.

We aim to acknowledge reports within 3 business days. Target fix times by severity: critical 7 days, high 30 days, medium 90 days.

Please do not test against other people's data or the hosted service without permission. A local `docker compose up` is the right place to test.
